🍎 OrchardMath

Backyard orchard math that holds up. Spacing, chill hours, bearing age, yield - planned for the tree it becomes, not the stick you planted.

Spacing & tree count

The nursery sells you a stick; the rootstock decides the tree it becomes. Spacing is set by the mature canopy, not the planting day.

Chill hours

Fruit trees count the cold hours under 45 F and refuse to crop without them. Your winter either covers the variety or it doesn't.

Bearing age & yield

Catalogs photograph the tenth year and price the first. Bearing age is a rootstock fact, and yield compounds with patience.

Pollination

Most apples and pears need a partner variety in bloom at the same time, close enough for a bee's commute.
